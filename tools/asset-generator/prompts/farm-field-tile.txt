Create exactly ONE seamless square terrain tile for a cute 2D farming arcade game.

STYLE:

Keep exactly the same cheerful polished cartoon farm-game visual language as the already approved Jake tractor and wheat collectible:

- bright friendly saturated colors
- lightly chibi
- lightly toy-like
- soft dimensional shading
- clean readable shapes
- professional casual mobile/web game quality

TERRAIN:

- warm brown cultivated farm soil
- clearly defined horizontal farming lanes / tracks
- mostly EMPTY playable ground
- subtle furrows and soil texture
- occasional tiny grass details only near lane edges
- very sparse decorative sprouts
- NO mature wheat rows
- NO large crop plants
- NO collectible-looking vegetables
- NO objects that could be mistaken for gameplay pickups

GAMEPLAY PURPOSE:

This tile will scroll underneath a moving tractor.

Later, separate game objects will be placed on top:

- wheat collectibles
- carrots
- pumpkins
- golden harvest
- sheep
- stones
- mud puddles
- other obstacles

Therefore the terrain must stay visually calm and readable.

The player's tractor and collectible objects must clearly stand out against the ground.

LANE STRUCTURE:

Create approximately 4 to 5 clear horizontal driving lanes.

Each lane should have enough open soil space for:

- one tractor
- collectibles
- obstacles

Use subtle darker furrows or small vegetation separators between lanes.

Do not fill the separators with large crops.

SEAMLESS / SCROLLABLE:

VERY IMPORTANT:

- visually seamless top-to-bottom repetition
- left and right edges should also feel naturally continuous
- no unique central landmark
- no border
- no rounded outer frame
- no vignette
- no dark edge shading
- similar texture density at opposite edges
- suitable for continuously scrolling terrain

CAMERA:

- elevated game-camera perspective
- mostly top-down
- only a slight three-quarter/isometric tilt
- no horizon
- no sky

STRICT REQUIREMENTS:

- square terrain tile
- opaque background
- mostly empty playable farm lanes
- no tractor
- no character
- no animals
- no wheat bundles
- no mature crop rows
- no carrot
- no pumpkin
- no barn
- no houses
- no signs
- no UI
- no text
- no logo
